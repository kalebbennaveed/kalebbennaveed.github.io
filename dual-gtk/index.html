The central idea

Learn the dynamics while completing the task.

Uncertain dynamics can make safe motion conservative. Deliberately informative motion can reduce that uncertainty, but it also takes time and effort. When should a robot explore?

Dual-gatekeeper treats exploration as a decision to verify before execution. The framework considers conservative and informative candidate trajectories, checks their safety and budget feasibility, and selects a feasible informative candidate using its predicted uncertainty reduction. Otherwise, it retains the backup policy.

Measurements collected during execution update the admissible model-parameter set through set-membership identification (SMID). The robot then replans with the updated uncertainty set. The same decision framework supports both deterministic and probabilistic safety verification.

Method overview

From candidate trajectories to a committed decision

  1. 01

    Generate candidate pairs

    Construct conservative and informative alternatives over candidate horizons using the current model estimate and uncertainty set.

  2. 02

    Verify safety and budget feasibility

    Check the prescribed safety conditions, including the transition to the backup or fallback policy, and reject candidates that exceed the remaining exploration budget.

  3. 03

    Commit an informative trajectory

    Rank feasible informative candidates by predicted uncertainty reduction, discounted by horizon length. Retain the backup policy when no suitable informative candidate is available.

  4. 04

    Update the uncertainty set

    Use the collected measurements to refine the SMID parameter set, update the plan, and repeat the decision process during the mission.

B

An explicit exploration budget

The budget limits the cumulative additional predicted mission cost of informative motion relative to its paired conservative alternative. Feasible candidates can reduce uncertainty while respecting this mission-level allowance.

Two safety instantiations

One decision framework, different verification methods

Deterministic · Drone navigation

Robust tube verification

Robust tubes bound closed-loop trajectories for all model parameters and disturbances within the stated bounds. An informative candidate must satisfy the constraints throughout execution and safely rejoin the backup tube.

Probabilistic · Autonomous racing

Sampling-based verification

Sampled closed-loop rollouts evaluate each candidate and its fallback transition. An exact binomial upper confidence bound is compared with the allowed failure tolerance under the assumed uncertainty distribution.

How racing candidates connect to the fallback policy +

Both conservative and informative racing candidates include their transition to a fallback policy. Each candidate policy is verified before commitment; nominal performance alone does not establish safety.
